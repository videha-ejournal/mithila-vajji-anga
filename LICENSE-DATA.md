## Exclusions

This data licence does **not** automatically relicense:

- book-cover art;
- reproduced manuscript or archival images;
- quoted third-party text;
- external map bases, editions, facsimiles, or datasets;
- source PDFs or book files unless their own rights statement permits reuse;
- trademarks or logos;
- source material whose own rights statement is more restrictive.

Those materials retain their original rights and attribution requirements. Machine-generated convenience translations are not treated as source-controlled scholarly editions.
