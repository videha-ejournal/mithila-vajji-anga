# Rights matrix

## Website code

Repository-authored software code is released under the MIT License in `LICENSE`.

## Original structured research data

Original normalized research data created for the **Videha Digital Research Archive — Digital Humanities Research Environment for Mithila, Vajji & Anga** is released under CC BY 4.0 as described in `LICENSE-DATA.md`.

## Original archive-level editorial text

Unless a page states otherwise, original explanatory text written specifically for the archive may be reused under CC BY 4.0 with attribution to Gajendra Thakur / Videha Maithili eJournal and the relevant page URL.

Long-form books, manuscript texts, translations, and other underlying works remain governed by their own publication or rights statements and are not automatically relicensed by the archive-level licence.

## Source PDF Library

PDFs published through `public/books/` are treated as source objects in the archive. The build may create a public catalogue entry, stable URL and SHA-256 checksum for each PDF, but this technical publication does **not** change the PDF’s copyright or licence. The rights statement governing the underlying book or document remains controlling.

## Images and reproduced material

Book covers, manuscript reproductions, archival images, quoted material, map bases, facsimiles, and external editions are excluded from the default archive data/text licence unless separately marked. Their source rights and attribution remain controlling.

## Machine-generated translation

Machine-generated convenience translations are access aids only. They are not source-controlled editions and should not be cited in place of the supplied authoritative text.

## Preferred attribution

Gajendra Thakur, Editor, Videha Maithili eJournal, ISSN 2229-547X, *Videha Digital Research Archive: Digital Humanities Research Environment for Mithila, Vajji & Anga*, followed by the permanent record or versioned dataset-release URL.
