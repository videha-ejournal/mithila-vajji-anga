## Release scope

This release preserves the bilingual Mithila–Vajji–Anga research environment and its source-controlled scholarly data. It includes the completed 178-chapter History corpus, 172 Parallel Philosophy source-verified chapters, the strict Panji source inventory, the authoritative 293-record ISBN register, source-PDF provenance, citation exports, historical-geography layers, accessibility engineering evidence, and versioned corpus checksums.

## Preservation rules

- Publication remains fail-closed: unresolved or synthetic source titles are not published as verified scholarship.
- Work-level ISBNs are not propagated to individual chapters unless the authority assigns them there.
- The ISBN source column `Name of Publishing Agency/Publisher` is excluded.
- Historical-geography candidates remain candidates until page-level geometry evidence is independently verified.
- Machine translation is not accepted as reviewed Maithili scholarship.
- Automated accessibility checks do not substitute for human NVDA/JAWS/VoiceOver/TalkBack testing.
- Repository-level creator, DOI and licence metadata describe the archival/dataset layer; item-level authorship, translation, editing, edition and rights notices remain authoritative.
