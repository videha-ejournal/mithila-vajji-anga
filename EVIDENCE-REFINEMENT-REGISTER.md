# Evidence refinement register

Status: **active scholarly maintenance register · 14 September 2026**

This register records research material that is known to exist but is **not yet strong enough to be promoted into a stronger scholarly claim**. Absence, uncertainty, and unresolved source structure are preserved explicitly rather than repaired by inference.

## Historical geography

Two History-PDF records are source-work candidates for historical geography. Candidate status does not establish a page-level map, boundary, coordinate, or geometry.

They remain **not usable for historical geometry** until all of the following are independently verified and source-controlled:

- exact page locator;
- stable source URL or source record;
- human-readable source citation;
- defined geometry scope;
- evidence status marked verified; and
- the uncertainty/precision qualifiers required by `HISTORICAL-GEOGRAPHY-POLICY.md`.

No automatic OCR, map guessing, interpolation, or visual resemblance may promote a candidate.

## Panji

The Panji pipeline continues to publish only source-verified titles and source structure. Unresolved chapter markers/titles remain omitted from permanent detail inventory rather than replaced with synthetic labels. Non-contiguous numbering is preserved as supplied by the source.

A Panji unit may move from unresolved to published only after its title/structure is verified against the source and the corresponding bilingual/editorial payload passes the archive gate.

## Bilingual detail pages

Collection-level bilingual architecture may exist before reviewed permanent detail text exists. A Maithili or English permanent detail page must not be generated merely to achieve route symmetry. Source-derived English material may be identified as English until reviewed Maithili scholarship is available.

Machine translation is not accepted as a substitute for reviewed Maithili research text.

## Accessibility evidence

Automated accessibility engineering checks are not equivalent to human assistive-technology certification. Keyboard-only, NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, TalkBack, zoom/reflow, contrast, text-spacing, reduced-motion, and touch-target results remain pending until they have actually been tested and recorded.

## ISBN and bibliographic evidence

The 293-record Videha ISBN authority is authoritative for ISBN assignment. ISBNs are attached only to the work/edition supported by that register. Parent-work ISBN context may be exposed for a constituent unit, but the ISBN must not be represented as belonging to an individual chapter unless the authority actually assigns it there.

The source column **Name of Publishing Agency/Publisher** remains excluded from the research archive's ISBN authority and generated metadata.

## Promotion rule

Any future promotion from candidate/unresolved/pending to verified/published/passed must be accompanied by source-controlled evidence that makes the change auditable. The project prefers a visible gap over an unsupported scholarly assertion.
